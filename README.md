# AXON — Architectural Terminology & Visual Anatomy Quiz Platform

An interactive architectural terminology and visual anatomy learning platform featuring **8 paired Francis D.K. Ching-style axonometric pen-and-ink plates**, **59 interactive anatomical hotspots**, **10 head-to-head diagnostic contrast pairs**, and a **12-domain self-study curriculum**.

## Features

- **01 • Interactive Visual Anatomy Quiz (59 Terms Across 8 Paired Plates)**
  - **Pinpoint on Plate (`Term → Tap Pin`)**: Tap the matching architectural component directly on the unlabelled axonometric drawing (with numbered or blind-dot difficulty).
  - **Reverse Visual ID (`Highlighted Pin → 4 Choices`)**: Identify the component marked by the pulsing `?` beacon on the drawing from 4 plausible terms (click/tap or keyboard `1`–`4`).
  - **Clue Blitz (`Definition → 4 Choices`)**: Match field-guide descriptions to architectural terms and see their locations revealed on the plate.
  - **Spaced-Repetition Mastery Queue**: Automatically tracks mastered terms in `localStorage` and provides an **Unmastered Queue** filter and **Shuffle** deck mode.
- **02 • Head-to-Head Diagnostic Contrast Pairs (`10 Classic Confusions`)**
  - Direct visual comparisons isolating `Pin A` vs. `Pin B` for *Mullion vs. Muntin*, *Engaged Column vs. Pilaster*, *Dome on Pendentives vs. Squinches*, *Mansard vs. Gambrel Roof*, *Ionic vs. Corinthian Capital*, *Oriel vs. Palladian Window*, *Keystone vs. Voussoirs*, *Vision Glass vs. Opaque Spandrel Panel*, *Vertical Mullion vs. Horizontal Transom*, and *Craftsman Bungalow vs. American Foursquare*.
- **03 • Paired Plate Visual Atlas (`8 Ching Axonometric Plates`)**
  - Switch seamlessly between **Interactive Pins**, **Hand-Lettered Reference Plate**, and **Side-by-Side Twin View**.
- **04 • 12-Domain Architectural Syllabus**
  - Searchable 120+ term curriculum across 4 progressive scales (Typologies & Styles, Massing & Classical Grammar, Openings & Tectonics, Ornament & Interiors).
- **Reversible Technical Ink & Mobile View**
  - Instant CSS/alpha-ink reversibility across **Vellum Ink** (light), **Dark Mode** (obsidian), and **Prussian Blueprint**, plus a responsive mobile layout and built-in **Mobile View** simulator.
